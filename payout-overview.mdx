A guide to integrating our payout API into your system

To integrate our Payout API into your system, follow the steps below:

1. List the payout method based on the country you want to process the payout to
2. Get the Payout metadata/fields that are required to process the payout
3. Create a beneficiary based on the payout metadata/fields
4. Finally, you can process the Payout
