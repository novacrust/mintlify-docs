Learn more about our card issuing service

1. **I tried creating a card, and the API returned “insufficient funds”**
   Answer: First, confirm that your Novacrust Merchant wallet has sufficient funds to handle the transaction. e.g., a USD card can only be created from a USD wallet balance
2. **I tried making a payment with my card, but it declined**
   Answer: Card transactions come with fees such as platform fees and fees that go to Mastercard/VISA, and they need to be accounted for. Sometimes, FX fees apply for transactions that are in currencies that are not the issued currency of the card. To make sure the transaction is successful, ensure you have more money on the card than the amount you want to pay. e.g., For a $$10 card payment, try to have $$13 in your card balance
3. **I withdrew from my card, but it is not in my USD wallet yet**
   Answer: Card withdrawals take 24 - 48 working hours to be processed into your wallet. So when you request, it goes into a pending state till it is processed.
