This page will help you get started with Novacrust Business API. You'll be up and running in a jiffy!

Novacrust provides a **RESTFUL API** based on simple **HTTP POST/GET** requests. Our Merchant APIs let you do the following:

1. Card Issuing & Management
2. Collection - Virtual Accounts, Crypto
3. Payouts - Bank Accounts, Mobile Money, Crypto, ACH/Wire

#### Integration

You can integrate the Novacrust API into your software applications and custom workflows to power your business operations.
